# Canonical Terminology

Santis OS uses a specific, standardized vocabulary to align the technical architecture with its Computational Hospitality vision.

- **User** → **Guest**: We serve guests, not standard users.
- **Cart** → **Itinerary**: Guests build itineraries, not shopping carts.
- **Checkout** → **Confirmation Ceremony**: The payment and booking process is a curated ceremony.
- **Admin Panel** → **Boardroom**: The backend management interface is the Boardroom, indicating governance and high-level control.
- **Booking Flow** → **Sovereign Booking Flow**: The end-to-end journey of reserving a service.

## Forbidden Vocabulary & Tone

To maintain the "Quiet Luxury" aura, the following language and copy patterns are strictly prohibited:

- **No SaaS language**: Avoid words like "dashboard", "onboarding", "metrics", "users", "platform", or "tenant" in guest-facing interfaces.
- **No startup jargon**: Avoid words like "disrupt", "hack", "leverage", "synergy", or overly casual terms like "awesome", "super", "cool".
- **No gamified UX language**: Do not use terms like "level up", "unlock", "achievements", or any copy that treats the luxury experience as a game.
- **No cheap conversion copy**: Avoid aggressive sales tactics like "Buy Now!", "Hurry!", "Limited Time Offer!", "Don't miss out!". Use elegant, calm, and confident invitations (e.g., "Reserve Your Sanctuary", "Discover More").
